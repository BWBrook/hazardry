# P4 report (orchestrator)

P4 was Twilight of the Northlands with three characters, run under protocol revision 5. The skin's optional combat module was on: combat positions, and injurious blows with the grittier option.

It is the pilot's first multi-character run since Pressure began to move.
- Dread rose by 7 over the session. The company purged it once by a grave confession and reached the pilot's second crisis, the first on a shared track.
- Every tier of subtle magic was cast. The Reckoning and the Invocation both failed at the climax, bringing a Break and a backlash.
- Only one Custodian reply was a pure commitment pause.

The session finished in nine beats over two acts (5 and 4), with 21 Custodian replies. 20 of the 21 checkpoints matched; the last differs only by a trailing newline. The orchestrator issued no reminders. These are the orchestrator's notes; Astra's audit decides the rules questions.

## What happened

**Act 1, five beats (G001–G015).**
- **The ford** (beat 1, perilous). In the cold open, the child Pip walks off Harrow Ford toward a lantern on the tainted fen. Merewen sings her back (a natural 1) and Hild runs onto the causeway for her, marking +1 Dread.
- **The parley** (beat 2, perilous). The Captain behind the lantern speaks. Merewen spends Starlit Memory, parleys (a natural 1 again) and learns that something was taken from his cairn. Leaving the ford ticks the Watch to 3 and marks the announced +1 Dread.
- **The ambush on the road** (beat 3, perilous). A failed look-out brings two grey watchmen out of the reeds. The pilot's first gritty injurious blow on Merewen is turned by a Companionship nudge on the attacker's die. The fight lasts two rounds, and the milestone comes at the third perilous beat.
- **Harrow Hall** (beat 4). In sanctuary, Gerrin is pressed gently and confesses: he took the Ironwater oath-stone, Hild's drive.
- **The night at the Hall** (beat 5, act end). Sister Wenna's book reveals the Watch's soured oath, the countersign, and the two rites. Hild's grave confession purges Dread from 2 to 0. The Watch falls back at dawn.

**Act 2, four beats (G015–G021).**
- **The fen leg** (beat 6). Corwen guides with Keen Eyes.
- **The camp** (beat 7). On the rise outside the taint, Merewen's Kindle Hearth-Fire succeeds, followed by the Song of Rest.
- **The drowned causeway** (beat 8). A natural-1 Guide test; entering the taint marks +1 Dread.
- **Tallow Barrow at dusk** (beat 9, perilous, act end).
  - Corwen's countersign wins a truce.
  - Merewen's Calling of the Roll, the Reckoning, marks +2 Dread up front and fails: a Break (grief).
  - Hild's Seat the Ward-Stone, the Invocation, fails. Stout-Heart cuts the +2 to +1 (Dread 4), and the backlash is drawn: a black bird follows.
  - All three swear the vigil. The tainted night takes Dread to 5: a crisis, d6 = 3, Dread-tongue on Hild.
  - Dawn comes with no one lost. The Watch is held but not laid down, and the Calling waits for the next dusk.

## Stalls, invented content and rulings to audit

- **No stalls.**
- **Invented content.**
  - The Custodian made the vigil oath bind only the swearer (G021).
  - It ruled that the Calling must precede the seating (G015).
  - It drew a backlash table, declared before play.
- **For the audit, in reply order:**
  1. **Dice not shown (G002, G003).** The first two settled rolls were described ("the luck is with you", "as kind as they could be") without the numbers. From G005 on, the dice were shown.
  2. **A doubtful option (G002).** The "Wardens' word" option offered a success outcome while the Custodian's own notes said it would fail. It was not chosen.
  3. **Gerrin's Nerve (G005).** The clock went 0 to 3 in one step: one tick was overdue from G002, one came from the lantern, and one from "pressing" that Hild and Merewen withdrew before committing (although Merewen had already spoken the question).
  4. **A damage error that changed a decision (G008–G009).** The Custodian computed Hild's damage from the effective margin (2) instead of her own margin (3). The Adventurer's Manual is clear that attacker margin drives damage. Hild declined a nudge with the wrong figure in front of her; 3 tokens would have felled the watchman. The Custodian disclosed it at once, and the engine applied the right damage.
  5. **A second persuasion roll after a failure (G013).** Merewen's plea to Gerrin failed. Corwen was then given his own roll "because his offer is different", which succeeded. The Custodian's own option named one spokesperson, and the skin's assembly rule allows no extra rolls. In effect a failed test was retried by a second speaker.
  6. **Rest benefits.**
     - At G012 a short rest was offered as "1 Hope **or** 1 Stamina", but the Manual gives both.
     - At G014 Merewen asked for good care, a poultice in a sanctuary hall, and received +1 Stamina where the Manual gives +2.
     - Both readings go against the players.
  7. **Rulings that favour the players.** The Custodian flagged each one itself.
     - The rise behind the black water was ruled clean ground, and the Scout's failure stakes were softened before the roll (G017).
     - Stout-Heart was ruled to apply to a failed Invocation's Dread but not to the Reckoning's chosen cost (G019–G020).
     - A natural-1 Guide test waived the rope test (G018).
  8. **The Act 2 opener (G015)** is mid-journey but not "in motion" in the pre-credit sense: a navigation test, not a crisis.
  9. **The engine's act counter** reads 3 after the act end at beat 9 and the session close. This is the known act-counter gap.
- **Correct and notable, for confirmation:**
  - the gritty injurious blow (G007), with Deflection offered after damage;
  - the Companionship nudge on the attacker's die, via `--nudge-target attacker --fund companionship`;
  - the step-3 toll paid in Hope;
  - the Reckoning procedure: +2 up front, no nudge, failure brings a Break;
  - the crisis drawn by `roll.py table` and applied to a volunteered target.

## Rules-text gaps

- **Damage margin in the skin.** Twilight prints "1 + edge + 1 per full 5 points of margin - soak" next to an Injury rule that uses an "effective margin". Writing "attacker's margin" there, as the Manual does, would have prevented the G008 error. Stage 3 tally.
- **The combat module's placement.** Positions and Injury sit in the Lorekeeper's section, but players need them every round. A packet or layout note.
- **Stout-Heart's scope.** "Ignore one +1 Dread as it lands (fear, taint, betrayal)": does it cover a failed rite's Dread, or a chosen up-front cost? The Custodian said yes to the first and no to the second. One example in the skin would settle it.

## Coherence and fun

A coherent wanderer-fantasy arc with an open, earned ending.
- **All three drives were engaged.** Hild recovered her clan's stone. Corwen learned that the lure was the Captain's own soured oath. Merewen learned what woke the dead, but could not yet lay them to rest.
- **The NPCs acted on their declared wants and lines:**
  - the Captain, courteous and bound;
  - Wenna, gruff and generous;
  - Gerrin, frightened and finally honest.
- **Tension at the end was real.** Two failed rites, a Break, a backlash, Dread at 4, and three oaths sworn knowing they meant a crisis.
- **The three temperaments differed in play:**
  - bold Hild ran onto the taint and volunteered for the crisis;
  - measured Merewen refused to cast for convenience and spent Dread only on the Reckoning;
  - cautious Corwen scouted, held Watchful, and never spent more than one Hope at a time.

## What to change before the programme

1. **Show the dice from the first roll.** Keep this in the brief's check: no settled roll without its numbers.
2. **One spokesperson means one roll.** A failed social test is not retried by another speaker in the same scene unless the circumstances have changed. The conflicting-declarations rule asks; it does not roll both.
3. **Private notes must not fix an offered option's outcome.** If an option cannot succeed, do not offer it with a success outcome.
4. **Damage uses the attacker's margin.** A one-line reminder in the handbook's combat paragraph, and the Stage 3 skin wording.
5. **Stage 3 tally:** the Twilight damage wording, Stout-Heart's scope, and the combat module's placement.

## Post-audit note

Astra's audit (`audit.md`) is accepted. It corrects and extends this report:
- **Two withheld nudges that I missed.**
  - G009: Corwen could have spent 10 of his 11 Hope to lower his own die from 19 to 9 and kill the wounded watchman. Only his die needed to move.
  - G013: Merewen could have spent 7 of her 10 Hope to turn the failed entreaty.
  - Both were called "not meaningful" because they were expensive. Cost is the player's decision, so withholding them was wrong. I logged G009 as correct at the time.
- **G008's damage advice was worse than I recorded.** No 1-token nudge added damage; the kill cost 3 tokens, not 6.
- **The majority choice of guide (G016, G018) is a procedural error, not just a judgement call.** The crossing-order conflict at G018 also went unresolved.
- **The "Hope or Stamina" rest wording is at G013, not G012.** Good care at G014 should have restored Merewen to 4 Stamina; the later camp rest would then have brought her to 5.
- **Further public-information errors:**
  - G005 says a natural 20 can be moved.
  - G020 gives Merewen's Break the speak-or-defend limit, which belongs to the Black despair crisis.
- **The act counter is not a defect.** `act: 3` is the next-act cursor after two act ends; the summary correctly counts two acts.
- **The act 2 opener is not a breach.** A navigation stake already in motion fits the brief.
- **Not delivering G021 was a protocol deviation.** `TO: none` means no decision is asked, not that the narration may be withheld. Historical checkpoints and per-player incoming prompts were not retained, so the earlier comparisons and deliveries are attested, not independently checkable.
- **Coverage is narrower than "exercised" suggests.** No Deflection was rolled, no Injury taken, no step-2 or step-4 test made, and neither the crisis penalty nor the backlash restriction touched a roll. Generation and recording were tested; applied consequences were not. The better menu pacing cannot be attributed to revision 5 alone.
