**What the pilot set out to answer** (protocol, Purpose):
- **Can a table of AI agents play a full session through the harness?** Yes, in all eight runs: solo and up to four characters, with a fresh-Custodian resume (P2), and on both platforms. Every run reached a session close, and no run needed a harness code repair mid-play. P6 needed a platform-limit increase.
- **What goes wrong?** Three things, which have changed from round to round:
  1. Passive, risk-avoiding tables (rounds 1–2).
  2. The cost of the stakes-before-commitment rule (round 3).
  3. Choices taken from players: wrong numbers, withheld spends, rolls selected or repeated (round 4).

  The harness itself held. Its gaps are a short backlog, listed below.
- **What does a session cost?** Where measured, Fable's Sonnet Custodians read 34 million (P3) and 22 million (P4) cached input tokens per session, and players 1.5–3.5 million each. Play took about 61 minutes (P3), 48 (P4), 43.5 (P7) and 20 (P8). Billed money is unavailable on both platforms.

## Observed under revision 5

**Threats moved and Pressure was announced.**
- In P4 all six Dread charge events (seven points) were named before they landed:
  - the Watch's sign-3 charge, one reply ahead;
  - tainted ground, before every crossing and camp;
  - "any further +1 will trigger a crisis".
- In P8 the twenty-minute Strain trigger was stated at the open and repeated exactly when it neared.
- Audits found no missed ambient charge in either run. These are two mixed-condition runs, not proof that revision 5 makes charging reliable.

**Options carried their consequences.** P4 had one separate commitment request and P8 none, against 4 and 8 in round 3. Most declarations were also the commitment.

**Pressure moved mostly where the scenario made the way forward cost it.**
- P4's Dread came from tainted ground on the only route, the Reckoning's price and the vigil. Before that, the players camped just outside the taint, paid costs in Hope and purged at the first sanctuary.
- P8's seven tests all had Advantage: six from Expertise, one from a paid Scout Surveyor, with the Watch benefit also applying to the trade. The crew left the buoy three minutes before the Strain trigger, after Rook spent Fate to make that possible.
- The AI players often chose ways to avoid Pressure while the fiction allowed it.

**Distinctive procedures were generated and recorded, but their consequences were rarely applied** (Astra's P4 qualification):
- every subtle-magic tier was rolled;
- the step-3 toll was paid;
- a backlash and a crisis were drawn, and a Break was recorded with a chosen impulse.

But no Deflection was rolled, no Injury taken, and the step-2, step-4, crisis and backlash penalties never touched a later roll.

## What went wrong: choices taken from players

Most of P4's adjudication errors, and both of P8's agency calls, share one pattern: the Custodian decided something that belonged to a player.

1. **Wrong numbers before a choice** (P4 G008). The damage was given from the wrong margin, so Hild chose with false prices.
2. **Expensive spends withheld as "not meaningful"** (P4 G009 and G013). 10 and 7 tokens, both affordable. The brief's exception covers only choices that cannot change the outcome.
3. **A failed test replaced by another character's roll** (P4 G013). "One spokesperson" was announced, and a second speaker was then given a roll.
4. **The actor chosen without asking** when players bid for the same role or roll:
   - P4 G016 and G018: chosen by majority;
   - P8 G003 and G005: chosen by best odds.
5. **A hidden cost applied to a withdrawn action** (P4 G005, Gerrin's Nerve).
6. **Public rules information missing or wrong** (P4):
   - no dice shown at G002 and G003;
   - "a natural 20 can move" (G005);
   - the Break given the Black despair limit (G020);
   - rest benefits under-read at G013 and G014.
7. **A doubtful option** (P4 G002): its advertised success was forbidden by the Custodian's private notes. Not chosen; not assessable.

**Scene granularity, again.** P8 split one continuous 17-minute operation into four beats, with a generous perilous flag, and that brought the milestone forward. This is the P1 pattern recurring.

**Records.**
- P4's closing body was not delivered to the players.
- P4 kept no historical checkpoints or per-player incoming prompts, so those claims are attested only.
- P4's last checkpoint has an extra newline.
- The append-only recap threads leave stale entries (P6, P7, P8).

## Proposals: protocol revision 6, for P9–P13

**A. Custodian brief: keep choices with the players.** These lines incorporate Astra's amendments (board 892).
- "Give players correct numbers before any choice. Damage uses the attacker's own margin; a skin's effective margin applies only to the thresholds it names."
- Keep the full post-roll check, and add: "Offer any affordable legal spend that changes the declared outcome or a meaningful degree of success, however costly, with its exact price. Cost is the player's decision. Check ability and payment choices as well as nudges."
- "When declarations genuinely need different actors for one roll or role, or are incompatible, ask the players to choose. Compatible help needs no new confirmation. Never choose the roller silently, by odds, or by an unagreed vote."
- "One spokesperson means one roll. Settle who speaks before rolling. Another character does not retry a failed test in the same scene unless the situation or the leverage has really changed."
- "An action withdrawn before commitment causes none of that action's consequences. Withdrawing it does not undo what was already done, or fictional time already spent."
- "Show the dice, the kept result and the margin for every roll."
- Extend the existing line: "Never offer an option whose advertised outcome your private notes rule out."
- "Keep Almanac 9's scene definition. Do not start a new beat merely because another test is called, or to refresh a power or reach a milestone."

**B. Orchestrator records.**
- Deliver every public body to every player, including the closing one (`TO: none` means no decision is asked).
- Keep a copy of each checkpoint version.
- Keep each player's exact incoming prompts.

Astra's P8 relay already does all three. Fable's `check_reply.sh` will copy `last.md` into `checkpoint_versions/` and log each outgoing relay.

**C. Scenario setup: honest, telegraphed dangers.** Astra's wording replaces my original "unavoidable Pressure price", which would have built the desired observation into every test (board 892).
- Give the scenario concrete, telegraphed Pressure-bearing dangers and consequential choices.
- Make alternatives carry honest fictional costs where appropriate.
- Keep clocks moving, and apply actual triggers even on success.
- A scene may have unavoidable exposure where its fiction warrants it, but it is not a quota for every run.
- If players avert the costs, record how. Never manufacture a charge to satisfy coverage.
- Procedures that ordinary play leaves unexercised go to separately labelled probes.

**D. Handbook lines,** taken from A, for any Custodian, not only the pilot:
- the meaningful-choice line;
- the one-actor and one-spokesperson lines;
- the attacker-margin reminder in the combat paragraph.

## Decision

**Revision 6.** Barry, 4 October 2026: "Approved, implement revision 6 once Astra's had a look." Astra's look (board 892) supported the rounds and the records, and amended A and C as incorporated above. In C, Astra's wording replaces Fable's original "unavoidable Pressure price".

**Free Traders Expertise.** Barry agreed with Astra's recommendation:
- remove the skin's broad-Expertise exception;
- keep the free Knack, and grant one ordinary narrow tag under the core rule, its scope fixed at creation (for example precision docking (DEX), jury-rigging damaged machinery (EDU), customs law (SOC));
- crew roles stay broad descriptions of competence, so routine role work needs no roll;
- a consequential role test uses the plain attribute unless the specialty, preparation or a paid Knack fits the actual method.

This is a rules change for the Stage 3 tally. Before adoption it gets a bounded, separately labelled Free Traders probe with P8 left frozen. The probe assesses whether Advantage, Fate spending and the Knack and Watch benefits become more meaningful. It does not require a crisis.
