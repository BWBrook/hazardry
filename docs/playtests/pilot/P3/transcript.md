## G001: Custodian

Night, 21 September 1924, a little before eleven. You stand on the iron gallery above the Sea-Gate cistern beneath the Dunmore Marine Station, your hooded lantern lit (Lantern 0/4). The air is cold brine and rot, and a buoy bell is ringing somewhere it should not.

Below you the cistern is filling. Black water is climbing the stone steps, already past the second stair, and from the far end comes a low, patient singing: six hooded townsfolk and a woman in a grey dress, Maud Pryor, her voice gentle as a lullaby. Between them, wrists bound in green kelp-cord, stumbles Ansel Pryor, whose note brought you here. Behind him walk two men with water streaming from their sleeves and no breath rising from their mouths.

"Come, dear heart," Maud tells Ansel. "The water only wants a name." They are walking him down to the fourth stair. At the pace of the rising water, you judge ten minutes, perhaps fifteen, before he is under it, and no one is looking up.

Within your reach: the iron stair down, the chalk in your pocket, and the Low Sluice winch beside your gallery, its wheel wet with salt. You have the Psalter, your knife, matches and the oil flask. Fate 10, Insanity 0, Stamina 6.

What you can try. All are risky, so stakes are stated now:

1. **Jam the winch** (VIG): success stalls the rising water and buys time; failure, the wheel shrieks, they look up, and you lose the quiet and a little of your skin (Lantern +1 in the scramble).
2. **Chalk the Sign of Warding** across the stair-arch below you (SCH, Rite): success, one of the wet men cannot willingly cross it until the scene ends, at the cost of 1 Fate or +1 Insanity; failure, +1 Insanity and the chalk wastes.
3. **Go down and cut him loose** (AGI or VIG, your call how): you reach him before the fourth stair, or you are seen, grabbed and put in the water beside him (Stamina at risk).
4. **Cry out** (FRT): the singers hesitate or they do not; if they do not, they know exactly where you are.
5. Anything else you can describe.

What do you do, and how?
