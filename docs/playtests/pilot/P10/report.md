# P10 report (orchestrator)

P10 was Service Duct Blues with three crew at the standard budget, under protocol revision 7. It is the first run of round 6. It is also the first test of the experimental fuse mapping: the frozen scenario said which core triggers feed Stress, and in what shipboard form.

**Summary:**
- **The episode worked as the skin intends.** A rescue cold open led into a technical A-plot: the dead overpressure sensor, the cascade and the Captain's deadline. Alongside it ran an interpersonal B-plot: a bridged trip, falsified scald charts and a framed petty officer. The two met in the Captain's ready room, and the stinger came over ration-pie in the mess.
- **Stress never moved.** The mapping priced every Stress-bearing temptation honestly, and the crew refused or averted each one. These were:
  - the fob override;
  - amending the log;
  - the corridor bribe;
  - the deadline lapse.
- **Revision 7's collision rules worked.**
  - Two actor collisions each cost one reply and settled at once. In each, the tie-break was offered and both players declined it.
  - Several other potential collisions never needed a pause, because the players named who should act in their own answers.

The run had 10 beats in two acts (6 + 4) and 20 Custodian replies. All 20 checkpoints matched. All 51 player deliveries were composed from the record and verified against what the players actually received. These are the orchestrator's notes; Astra's audit decides the rules questions.

## What happened

**Act 1, six beats (G001–G015).**
- **DUCT-K9** (beat 1, perilous). In the cold open, Petty Officer Quill is trapped behind a surge seal that is counting down. Sefa's Quick Scan finds the overpressure trip never firing. Imre calls "Reroute!" (SYS with Advantage) and shunts the pressure into Loop 2. Jun's crawl is not needed. Behind the panel is an unlogged jumper clip tagged ENG-D4, bridging the trip. Imre photographs it, bags it and logs it honestly. When he reports it, Lt. Cmdr Brevik goes quiet for a second (Clean Slate +1, private) and asks for the clip.
- **MED-C1** (beat 2). Quill's airway is closing. Sefa keeps him awake (HAR success), and her Quick Scan rolls a natural 1. It shows Loop 3 coolant dye in Quill's scalds and in two "galley scald" charts, one nine days old. Quill admits he fitted the clip ("I was told") but will not say who told him. Imre's pressing fails by 5, and he declines the 5-chit nudge. Quill is sedated.
- **MED-C1, a quarter hour on** (beat 3). The Captain announces her deadline, with its mapped Stress price stated. Brevik asks Imre to amend his entry to "seal wear", which the mapping prices at +1 Stress. Imre refuses, and wins a witnessed handover with Dace present (HAR 12 against 12). Sefa and Jun stop Pell editing the charts, with no roll.
- **Deck 9 aft** (beat 4, perilous). Imre and Sefa both claim the Quick Scan; one reply settles it in Imre's favour. The scan names a dead HX-40 transducer. Jun closes the K9 cutoff, using Cross-Training Override (Ops/Security, 1 RES).
- **MESS-D9** (beat 5). A galley-closed notice is signed by Pell for Brevik. Sefa talks the yeoman down, and Mess Chief Marsh trades her spare HX-40 for a logged statement, ruled a fair favour.
- **ENG-D4** (beat 6, act end). Imre and Sefa both claim the call to the Captain; one reply settles it in Imre's favour. Imre fails by 3 and pays 3 chits, then makes the swap with "Reroute!". The live trip shuts Loop 3 down, the second bell rings over a stable plant, and the rendezvous slips. Then Clean Slate fills: Pell's archive "reconcile" names Quill as the signing inspector, and the Captain orders him confined. Act 1 ends on that cliffhanger.

**Act 2, four beats (G016–G020).**
- **MED-C1** (beat 7). The players secure Quill under a medical hold, and the Captain freezes the audit trail on logged evidence. Imre's read-only pull, witnessed by Dace, shows the entries were rewritten eleven minutes after his call, under Pell's credential with Brevik's code. In the corridor, Brevik offers the Tessaly roster, which is Jun's drive, in exchange for a day's silence. The offer is priced as an off-book deal at +1 Stress. All three refuse, Jun included. Imre's counter-offer, that Brevik report himself, succeeds.
- **The Captain's ready room** (beat 8). Brevik falters back into his rehearsed account. Imre's prompt fails by 5, and he declines the all-in nudge. He lays the evidence out himself. Brevik is relieved, Pell is detained and Quill is cleared.
- **ENG-D4 and sickbay** (beat 9). Dace rates Imre's work spotless, though there is no Stress to purge. Okoro gives Jun the Tessaly seat for his honest report. Sefa's scan rolls a natural 1 and finds the galley clean, and she restores seven mis-charted burns.
- **MESS-D9** (beat 10, act end). The stinger: Quill eats his first meal, and a dead array chirps on an eleven-minute interval.

## Revision 7 against its aims

**1. Actor collisions: fixed in practice.**
- There were two pure pauses in 20 replies (G009 and G013), against six in P9's 27.
- Each time, the Custodian asked the claimants to choose and offered the opt-in tie-break in the same reply. Both times the players converged on one actor in a single round and declined the die.
- The player brief's new line was used from the first turn. In P001 all three players volunteered deference or tie-break terms, and in P011 all three named Sefa for one roll without being asked.
- The two tie-break seeds that were reserved but not used passed to the next real roll, so the schedule had no gaps.

**2. Ability offers: much improved, with one open question.**
- Before every roll the Custodian offered optional powers at their cost: "Reroute!", Cross-Training Override, Quick Scan and Deep Scan.
- It announced when once-per-scene uses had refreshed. It applied both natural-1 extras.
- It offered every affordable nudge at its exact price: 5, 3 and 5. The first and third were declined. The 3 was paid.
- **Open question: help.** The Custodian gave no Advantage when a companion backed a social roll as a second witness (G005–G006, G014, G017). It said nothing either way, whereas P9's Custodian gave Advantage for bracing a rope. The Manual leaves this to the Custodian, but revision 7's correct-numbers rule implies the effect should be stated before the roll.

**3. Private material: clocks kept private; one borderline case.**
- The Custodian never named Cascade or Clean Slate in public. It showed them only through signs: the channel silence, Pell's tablet, the galley-closed notice.
- **Borderline (G017, option 2).** The text says the Captain "acts on logged evidence at once". That is her private line stated as a fact. It had already been shown in play at G016.
- **Relay-status leak (G003).** The public text repeated my note on who had seen what, a minor and harmless leak of relay status.

**4. Scenario fidelity: clean.**
- The timetabled ticks fired on schedule, including those that do not depend on failure.
- The frozen damps were applied as written.
- The seeds were continuous, and the campaign copy of the scenario is unchanged.

**5. Perilous flags: strict, perhaps too strict.**
- Only beats 1 and 4 were flagged, so no milestone came this session.
- **Beat 6 is the case to audit.** The time-sensitive swap carried the goal, and failure meant worse pulses and a missed deadline. Under revision 7's wording, "failure at its consequential choice could cost … the goal", it arguably qualifies.
- After P12's generous flags, P10 may show the opposite error.

**6. Orchestration: verified.**
- The relay script removed P9's omission class: all 51 deliveries matched what the players received.
- The packet preflight and the seed check both passed.

## Pressure and the fuse mapping

The experimental mapping did what it was designed to do. Generic danger never fed Stress: the flash-burn risks, the scalding steam, an airway closing, a timer counting down. Only scrutiny, procedure and deadlines could. The players used it as a working rule:
- Jun cited "a promise we can't keep is an off-book deal" (P011).
- Imre asked before committing whether a private call counted as "in the public eye" (P012). The Custodian ruled it did not.

**But Stress ended at 0, as Sin did in P9 and Pressure did in P8.** Every mapped exposure was telegraphed with an honest alternative, and the crew took the alternative every time:

| Temptation | Price | Avoided by |
|---|---|---|
| Fob override at the hatch (G001) | +1 (interlock) | Reroute! |
| Amend the log to "seal wear" (G007) | +1 (breaking the log) | Refusal |
| Strip a hydroponics line (G010) | priced on request | Marsh's spare |
| The second-bell deadline (G007–G015) | +1 once per act | The swap, made in time |
| Brevik's corridor deal (G016) | +1 (off-book deal) | Refusal and counter-offer |

**What this suggests:**
- Narrowing the triggers makes Stress more thematic. It also makes it easier to avoid, because no exposure was unavoidable.
- With honest AI players and telegraphed prices, a skin-distinctive fuse moves only where:
  - the scenario makes some exposure unavoidable in its fiction; or
  - the honest route has a real cost the players will sometimes refuse to pay.
- P12's Anomaly filled from unavoidable time under threat, and that is exactly the trigger this mapping narrowed.

This is a scenario-design point for the Custodian, not evidence against the mapping. The joint review should weigh P13's mapping alongside it.

## Coherence and fun

- **Coherence.** The episode was coherent and on theme. The A-plot and B-plot alternated as the skin's Showrunner notes ask, and the act break came at a real cliffhanger.
- **Temperaments drove the choices:**
  - Imre's log line decided three scenes.
  - Sefa's patient line kept her at Quill's bed through the climax.
  - Jun's bold drive was put on offer (the roster) and refused on principle.
- **NPCs.** They had wants and limits, shown through signs: Brevik's tremor and relief, and Pell giving up without a fight.
- **Tension.** The episode felt safe. No character lost Stamina, and no roll set off a cascade. That suits the skin's "optimism with friction", but it shows the same low-jeopardy pattern as P8 and P9.

## What to change before the programme

- **Help.** A brief line asking the Custodian to state whether a companion's help gives Advantage before the roll. This applies the correct-numbers rule; it is not a new rule.
- **Perilous flags.** Revision 7's wording may read too narrowly. An example in the brief would help: "a time-sensitive repair on which the goal depends is perilous".
- **Private clocks.** When one is about to fill, offer at least one of the frozen interrupts as an option before it fills, so the clock's signs can be acted on.
- **Fuse mappings.** Should state which exposures are unavoidable, and give the honest route a real cost. Otherwise honest players keep Pressure at 0. This is for the joint review with P13.
- **Stage 3 tally:** add the help effect; Luck paying for a companion's roll continues from P9.
- **Harness.** Keep `relay.py` and `seeds.py`, which together removed the relay-omission and seed-reuse classes from P9.
