
## G001: Custodian

Dusk, the ford shin-deep and ten paces wide, the cart axle-deep in the near shallows. Dread stands at 3.

**The fight begins.** Initiative: orcs 15, company 5. The orcs act first every round, and the order holds all fight.

**Round 1 positions.** The orcs have declared: both spearmen Steady, the archer Ranged, Grishnar Vanguard (Advantage on attacks, Disadvantage on defence). The spearmen's spears point at the cart. Each of you now declares:

2. Your position: Vanguard, Steady, Watchful or Ranged.

Standing in the ford or at the cart engages any foe there. An orc at the cart and unengaged works it loose instead of attacking. Four such rounds and the cart is theirs (Cart Taken, 0/4); a companion engaging every orc at the cart stops the count for that round.

**Prices you should know.** Every attack, and any other test whose failure matters, is a risky test at Dread 3: it costs 1 Hope before rolling, or marks +1 Dread. Dread 4 puts Disadvantage on all tests; Dread 5 is a crisis. Defence rolls and Deflection tests carry no toll. Your Companionship pool is 3/3, and you may nudge once per scene each from it instead of your own Hope.

**Their defences:** spearmen parry with STR 11, riveted mail soak 1; the archer dodges with NIM 11, leather soak 0; Grishnar parries with STR 12, hauberk and shield soak 2.
