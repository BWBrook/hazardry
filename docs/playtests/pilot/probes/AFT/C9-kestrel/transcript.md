
## G001: Custodian

Beyond the Wayward Sun's non-rotating exterior truss, a loose case drifts into open zero-G. Rook is already crossing to secure it; the gap is widening.

Rook, your DEX is 10. You can use Salvage Rat once this scene: pay 1 Fate (11 to 10) or mark +1 Strain (0 to 1) to secure the case automatically, with no roll. Zero-G adds no extra Strain. Or decline it and make a plain DEX test: success secures the case; failure loses it and costs you 1 Stamina (6 to 5). That failure consequence follows from the roll. Which do you choose?
