
## G001: Custodian

Mercy Reach’s freight counter is already processing the Wayward Sun’s private carriage of the certified incubators. The delivery acknowledgement flashes green; the payment line is still blank. A warning earned on the crew’s earlier Watch reaches Mira as the settlement window opens.

Mira is committed to broker this one unpaid freight job with SOC 13. The Watch warning grants Advantage on this first encounter test: roll 2d20 and keep the lower. Customs law does not apply. Success consumes the stake and raises Ship Shares from 2 to 3. Ordinary failure consumes it, gains no Share and marks +1 Strain. A natural 20 consumes it, gains no Share, marks +1 Strain, ticks Debt +1 and brings an audit complication. There is no upfront Strain charge. Fate decisions follow the dice; Merchant Broker remains available after the primary result.

## P001_mira_quill: Mira Quill

“I’m settling the incubator freight account. The delivery is acknowledged; now I want the payment entered. Let’s see what the dice say.”

## G002: Custodian

The Watch warning gives Mira two dice: 9 and 15. She keeps 9 against SOC 13, a success with margin +4. The primary test is settled. No Fate nudge changes this trade’s stated outcome or a stated degree of success, so none is spent. The freight stake and payment remain unresolved until Mira decides on Merchant Broker.

Mira may keep this success: the freight stake is then consumed and Ship Shares rise from 2 to 3. Or she may use Merchant Broker once this port call to reroll the same test with Watch Advantage. It costs either 1 Fate (10 to 9) or +1 Strain (0 to 1), paid before the reroll. The new result replaces this success, even if worse: success pays +1 Share; ordinary failure pays no Share and adds +1 Strain; a natural 20 also ticks Debt +1 and brings an audit complication. Which does Mira choose?

## P002_mira_quill: Mira Quill

“I’ll take the payment as settled. One Share keeps us moving, and I won’t gamble the crew’s earnings on a second throw.”

## G003: Custodian

Mira keeps her 10 Fate. Strain and Debt remain at 0. The Watch warning has served this test and is cleared.
