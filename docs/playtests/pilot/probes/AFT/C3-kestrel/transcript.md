
## G001: Custodian

The Wayward Sun's coolant manifold splits along its seam. A bright line of fluid spits across the deck; the pressure needle climbs toward rupture.

Rook has the scrap and engineering kit needed to reach the break and is committed to jury-rigging it. Test EDU 10 with Advantage from Engineer (EDU): roll 2d20 and keep the lower. Success stabilises the manifold. Failure adds 1 Hull Damage and forces shutdown. The tools give access, with no further bonus; this is not a jump-role test. No Strain charge applies before the attempt.

## P001_rook_fen: Rook Fen

“I trace the spray back to the seam, brace the scrap across it, and set the clamps with the engineering kit. Hold steady, Wayward Sun.”

## G002: Custodian

Rook's clamps bite into the scrap as the coolant jet shakes the line. Engineer (EDU) gives Advantage: the dice are 13 and 14; keep 13. Against EDU 10, that is margin −3, a failure unless Fate changes it.

Rook can spend exactly 3 Fate to move the kept 13 to 10, stabilising the manifold and leaving 8/11 Fate. Or spend none: Hull Damage rises by 1 and the ship must shut down. Which does Rook choose?

## P002_rook_fen: Rook Fen

“I spend 3 Fate. The clamp slips, but I catch the scrap on the next vibration and drive it tight across the seam. Keep the ship running.”

## G003: Custodian

The scrap patch holds across the seam. Coolant pressure falls out of the red, and the Wayward Sun stays running.

Rook spent 3 Fate to move the kept die from 13 to 10: EDU 10, final margin 0, success. Rook has 8/11 Fate. Hull Damage remains 0/6, Strain 0/5, and Ship Shares 2. The manifold is stabilised; no shutdown occurs.

## FINAL_rook_fen: Rook Fen

“Patch is holding. I’ll keep an eye on that seam.”
