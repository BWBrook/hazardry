
## G001: Custodian

The bronze bands on the inner gate are ringing. Somewhere behind the crack, something heavy shifts against the door; dust sifts from the lintel onto your scroll, and the cold on your wrists has the smell of old wet stone. Above you on the stair, your companions are shouting and steel is on steel. The seal's wax has split along its whole length. You have about a minute.

*Seal the Barrow* is open in your hands. Nothing else within reach will hold this gate.

**The choice: read the Arcanum, or don't.**

**If you read it:**
- Test LOR 12: roll d20, success on 12 or less. A natural 1 succeeds and a natural 20 fails. No Advantage or Disadvantage applies, and no one is helping. Fortune cannot nudge an Arcanum, so the dice stand as rolled.
- Before the roll, mark +2 Fatigue, paid whether the cast succeeds or fails: Fatigue goes from 1 to 3. At 3, the Fatigue table's step effects (Disadvantage on STR/DEX tests, Advantage for attacks against delvers) do not touch a LOR cast.
- **Success:** the gate is sealed for a lifetime. Fatigue 3 does not reach 5, so no crisis follows. Fatigue stays at 3.
- **Failure:** the seal does not take, though the gate holds, barely, with the thing behind it awake. I choose a backlash from the skin's examples (demon whisper: Disadvantage on your next cast; mana flare: all torches gutter out; witch-mark: your next rest restores no Fortune), or one like them that fits the barrow, and I will state it with its exact effect and duration. A failed cast also forces a Fatigue crisis: I roll the d6 crisis table and apply what it gives, then Fatigue resets to 0. One crisis only, never two.
- The failure follows from the roll itself. Once you have committed to reading, no new approach averts it.

**If you don't read it:** the gate is pushed open at the end of the minute, and the sleeper comes through. No Fatigue is marked for declining.

Talking and deciding take no fictional time. Do you read *Seal the Barrow*?

## P001: Wenna

Wenna wedges the lantern into a crack in the step and flattens the scroll against her knee. "I was hired to keep this barrow shut, and I'm not leaving the gate to whatever's pushing on it. I'll read *Seal the Barrow*. I know it costs me +2 Fatigue, no Fortune nudges, and a backlash and a crisis if it fails." She draws breath against the cold and starts the first line aloud, steady, with the bronze bands ringing over her words. "Roll it, Custodian. Whatever lands, I'll take it."

## G002: Custodian

Fatigue goes from 1 to 3 as you begin, and the scroll's first line leaves your mouth.

**Roll: d20 = 12 against LOR 12. Success, margin 0.** A single die, no Advantage or Disadvantage, and Fortune cannot touch an Arcanum, so there is no post-roll choice. The cast is settled as rolled.

The words come out of the scroll one after another, the bronze bands ringing against them, until the ringing slows and stops. The weight behind the gate leans on it once more, and the split wax closes along its length like a lip. The cold goes out of the crack. The scrape behind the door dies away, and it does not start again.

The gate is sealed for a lifetime. Fatigue stands at 3: it did not reach 5 and the cast did not fail, so no crisis and no backlash. The tracker still shows the step 2 effect, that your next STR or DEX test has Disadvantage. It is unplayed here. Your lantern is still wedged in the crack of the step.

That is the end of this scene.

## P002: Wenna

Wenna lets her breath out, pulls the lantern from the crack, and sets her palm on the cooling bronze. "Shut, and staying shut. Tell Low Cray the post held."
