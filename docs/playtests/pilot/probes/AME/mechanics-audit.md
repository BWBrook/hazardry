# AME independent mechanics audit

Scope: the completed M0 original, M1 replacement R2, M2 original after documented transport recovery, and M3 original. I compared the accepted [probe specification](../../../docs/playtests/pilot/probes/mapped-exposure.md) with each case's transcript, command ledger, final event log, procedure ledger, and collected closeout checks. M1 original was an incomplete harness attempt and M2 R2 was unlaunched; neither supplies play evidence. This audit concerns reached mechanics, not approval of the proposed general mapping.

The command ledgers and `collection_v2/final/state/logs/session_001.jsonl` support the charges, rolls, settlements, crisis and reset above. The final procedure ledgers preserve M1's per-PC pending flag, M2's paid toll, and M3's lasting restriction. In all four cases, `collection_v2/closeout_checks.json` reports complete disposition, normal stop, byte-equal received public bodies and checkpoints, one public-log occurrence per GM body, and equal saved delivery inputs. Several terminal receipts use `Received.` rather than literal `Received`; the collector accepted them. M2's recovery is specifically recorded and should remain attached to any reproducibility claim.

These cases demonstrate one live mapped exposure, the scripted thresholds at 2/4/5, the Fortune-paid step-4 branch, and one face-3 crisis continuation. They do not test the dry M0 outcome, a Fatigue-paid action toll, crisis recursion, other terminal faces, or ordinary-session frequency. The M1 late help ruling is a concrete procedure error; M3's timing is a judgement call. Neither is evidence of a wrong pressure arithmetic result in the reached branches.
