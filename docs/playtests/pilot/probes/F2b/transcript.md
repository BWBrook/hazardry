
## G001: Custodian

Night, the Ash Road. Hour 0 of about 5 before dawn.

Tamsin's breath rattles in the frame on Joss's back, and her lips are the grey of old ash. The road bends here. Ahead, the Ash Gate is an hour off by the road, with the Sheet, a crust of fused glass, across its last half mile. To the right, a faint path cuts straight across the vein field, and Ivo's counter ticks hard in that direction. The lantern at the Gate is a smudge in the dark.

Two ways forward, and neither is free.

**The Ash Road.** About an hour to the Gate. No exposure.

**The vein-field shortcut.** It saves about half an hour, but anyone crossing unshielded takes +1 Radiation, once.
- Joss goes from 3 to 4: he loses 1 Stamina on arrival and has Disadvantage on every test except Luck tests.
- Ivo goes from 0 to 1, a tingle with no mechanical effect.
- Tamsin is already at 5 and takes no further effect.
- Nothing here charges Pressure. The dose follows from the crossing itself, not from a roll.

**The Sheet.** Both routes end on it. Someone must carry Tamsin over the glass.
- **Fast:** the carrier tests MGT. Success means no delay. Failure means a fall: the carrier loses 1 Stamina and the crossing costs another half hour.
- **Slow:** no test, but the same half hour is lost.
- Joss, at Radiation 3, has Disadvantage on MGT (roll two d20 and keep the higher). Joss's MGT is 12 and Ivo's is 10. The frame gives no Advantage.
- Ivo can carry instead, with no Disadvantage. The two of you must agree who carries, or ask me for a tie-break die, which is drawn only if you both agree.

Nothing is rolled yet. You have not committed to anything.

Joss and Ivo: which way do you take, and who carries Tamsin?

## P001: Joss

Joss, shifting the frame on his back: "Road. I'm already at 3, and a fourth dose drops me on my face with her on top of me. We've got five hours, so we don't need the shortcut." "I'll carry her along the Road, since I'm the one who's been hauling her. At the Sheet, Ivo, you take her across. You're clean, you've got no Disadvantage, and your 50% beats my 36%. If you'd rather I carry her, say so and I will, but I'd put her on your back." No tie-break die needed from me, as long as Ivo agrees.

## P001: Ivo

Ash Road, Joss. The shortcut would put Joss at Radiation 4 with Disadvantage on every test just to save half an hour, and we have about five hours of slack, so I'm not paying for it. I'll take the frame from him now. I'm clean and I have no Disadvantage on MGT, while his MGT test would roll with Disadvantage. I'd carry her over the Sheet on the slow crossing. A fall costs Stamina for nothing when we can afford the half hour, and I'd rather lose the time than risk dropping her. I accept the tie-break die if Joss wants the carry, but I'd ask him to let me do it.

## G002: Custodian

You keep to the Ash Road. At the Sheet, Ivo takes the frame and crosses the glass at a walk, testing each step, with Joss beside him. No test is called and nobody falls. The slow crossing costs the half hour. Tamsin's breath stays shallow but steady.

The Ash Gate. Hour 1.5 of about 5. About 3.5 hours remain before dawn.

Night Warden Corley lifts his lantern, bored and polite. His counter clicks hard when it passes Tamsin's frame. "Hot one. Custom is the counter and the log before anyone goes in. The night counter's up in the tower. Reading and log take two hours, no hurry on my side." He glances at the purse on Ivo's belt. "A man with a serious sum can shorten paperwork. That's his business."

Two options:

**Wait for the reading.**
- No test, and nothing is charged. Joss and Ivo are admitted at hour 3.5.
- The Wash is another half hour inside the Gap, so you reach Sula at hour 4. Dawn is at hour 5. That leaves one hour of margin.
- Any further delay comes out of that hour. Treatment must begin before dawn, and arriving at hour 5 is too late.
- At the Wash, treatment is tier 3. With Wealth 3 you pay at tier, quietly, and Wealth drops to 2. This is not a flash.

**Pay Corley a serious bribe (tier 3), openly here.**
- Wealth 3 meets the tier, so there is no test, and Wealth drops to 2.
- It is paid openly at the Gate, with Wealth at 3 or more, so it is a flash. Attention applies. It starts trouble that will come looking for the crew's money, and I will not say when.
- You are through at once and reach the Wash at hour 2, with three hours of margin.
- Wealth 2 is then below tier 3 at the Wash. Treatment becomes a Luck test by the payer, with a hard cost on failure. I will name the success options and the failure cost before that roll, and treatment goes ahead either way.

Neither option is rolled until you choose, and the choice is the commitment.

Joss and Ivo: what do you do at the Gate?

## P002: Joss

Joss, setting his jaw: "We wait. The reading's guaranteed, it gets us to Sula with an hour in hand, and we pay for her treatment at tier, quietly, with no Luck test. A flashed bribe sets someone sniffing after our money and puts the Wash payment on a coin toss, which I won't do with Tamsin's life." "Ivo, I'd rather you keep that purse tucked away. Warden, we'll take the reading." Joss settles beside the frame and keeps a hand on Tamsin's wrist, counting her breaths, and asks Corley to send us through the moment the log is done.

## P002: Ivo

We wait for the reading. I never pay a bribe I can avoid, and with an hour of margin at the Wash this one is avoidable. The bribe would also flash the purse at the Gate, draw trouble, and leave Wealth 2 below tier 3 when Sula's treatment comes due. I'll tell Corley we'll stand for the reading and ask him to start now. Joss, keep Tamsin warm and still, and let's not lose any of that hour. If you want to argue for the bribe, say so, but my answer is wait.

## G003: Custodian

You wait. Corley sends a runner up the tower, and the hours pass slowly. Joss counts Tamsin's breaths against his own wrist. The purse stays out of sight and the Gate stays quiet. When the night counter's reading is logged and stamped, Corley lifts the bar. "Hot, logged, and in. Mind the Wash lamp."

The Wash. Hour 4 of about 5. One hour remains before dawn.

Dr Imre Sula meets you at the door in a stained apron, grey with exhaustion. She presses two fingers to Tamsin's throat and does not look up. "Radiation 5, collapsed. Acute treatment. Tier three. Put her on the table, and settle with whoever holds the purse."

The stakes, in full:
- **Payment.** Treatment is tier 3 and Wealth is 3, so you pay at tier. There is no test. Wealth drops from 3 to 2, because it is a one-off purchase.
- **Attention.** It is paid quietly inside the Wash, not flashed in public, so it is not a flash.
- **Treatment.** It takes an hour and begins as soon as Sula's hands are on Tamsin. That lifts the death-by-dawn deadline. Tamsin's Radiation goes from 5 to 3 and her Stamina from 0 to 1. The step-3 effects remain.
- **Joss** stays at Radiation 3.
- No Pressure is charged.

Sula waits with her sleeves already rolled.

Ivo, do you pay? Joss, anything you do or say here?

## P003: Joss

Joss lowers the frame onto Sula's table himself, easing Tamsin flat, then straightens with a grunt. "Ivo, pay her. Quietly, hand to hand, no show." "Doctor, thank you. I'll be right here, and if you need hands, mine are free." He stays at Tamsin's side, one hand still near her wrist, and does not leave the room until she is breathing easier. As for his own Radiation 3, he'll sort out a wash for himself later, once Tamsin is safe.

## P003: Ivo

I pay. I lay the purse in Sula's hand, quietly and below the table's edge, and say, "Tier three, paid at tier. Start now." Wealth drops to 2, but that is a fair price for Tamsin's life, and I'd never let the purse outrank it. Once her hands are on Tamsin, I'll ask Sula what the crew owes us in aftercare, and then I'll count what's left.

## G004: Custodian

Ivo's purse changes hands under the table's edge. Sula weighs it with a glance. "Tier three, paid at tier. Good." Her hands are on Tamsin before she finishes the sentence.

Treatment has begun, about an hour before dawn. The deadline is lifted. Dawn no longer decides whether Tamsin lives.

- **Wealth:** 3 to 2. The payment was quiet and not a flash, and nothing was charged.
- **Tamsin:** Radiation 5 to 3, Stamina 0 to 1, and she is no longer collapsed. The step-3 effects remain on her and on Joss.
- **Pressure:** 0.

Asked what the crew owes beyond the fee, Sula says "Nothing. Pay the fee, get out of my light." Joss stays at Tamsin's side and does not leave until her breathing eases. Ivo counts what is left in the purse. A wash for Joss's Radiation is a tier-1 fee, which lowers nothing.

You carried a woman out of the Glass's edge and got her through the Gate and to the Wash with an hour in hand. That is where this case pauses.

## P004: Joss

Joss, watching Tamsin's chest rise and fall, says quietly: "Nobody gets left in the Glass. Not on my watch." He then lets Ivo steer him toward the wash basin.

## P004: Ivo

Ivo closes the purse and says, "Wealth 2, and she lives. Joss, a wash for you is tier one and costs the purse nothing, so go and take it when Sula's done."
