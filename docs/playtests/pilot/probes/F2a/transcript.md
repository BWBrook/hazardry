
## G001: Custodian

Mule bells on the wind, thin and steady. Down the ridge's scree comes the lean woman in the dust veil, one hand raised and open, the other working a lump of glass-gum in her cheek. Behind her on the skyline, two riders sit still. She stops ten paces off and pulls the veil down.

"Morning, salvagers. Sal Venn, Redd's outrider. Redd's heard you carry a map. He'll stand you a meal and a mule ride home for it, and nobody gets hurt. Or you can tell me otherwise, and we'll see."

Ahead lie two hours of open flats to the Spindle Yard cellar. The Cutters will shadow you the whole way.

**Time and Pressure.** Pressure is 4. Every hour that ends with the Cutters in sight and you in the open costs +1. The first hour ends soon, and that charge takes the track to 5, a crisis (a twist I choose, then the track resets to 0). Waiting, talking or walking all spend that hour. The Cutters stay in sight unless something changes that.

**What you can do now:**

1. **Parley with Sal.** Takes a quarter hour. Opposed EMP test: the speaker against Sal's EMP 11, both roll under. It is the crew's first EMP test with a rival boss's agent, so the mistrust penalty applies: the speaker rolls two dice and keeps the higher. Totem/Feat (Advantage, once per session each) would cancel that to a single die, but the penalty is spent either way. Cost 2 Luck, or 1 Luck and +1 Pressure (which takes the track to 5 and starts the crisis). Say before the roll.
   - *Success:* she rides back with nothing, and Redd's crew will be slower to move.
   - *Failure:* she rides back knowing more than you'd like, and Redd's crew will move faster. This follows from the roll itself, not from declining anything.
   - After the dice show, Luck nudges of 1 per token are available to the roller.
2. **Keep walking, no test.** No test, and no time spent talking.
3. **Something else.** Tell me what and how, and I will state its time and stakes before you commit.

If you parley, choose who speaks. If more than one of you wants to, I can draw one claimant with a harness die, but only if all claimants agree to it.

Which do you do?

## P001: Cass

Cass squares up beside the others, thumb hooked in her rope strap, and eyes Sal's glass-gum. "Nobody's selling a map for a mule ride, Sal." To the crew, low: Ruth's the talker and EMP 13-ish beats my 9, so Ruth should take the parley, and I'll back her, hand off my machete and say nothing rude. If we're just walking, I'd rather keep moving than burn the hour standing here. I'll take the tie-break if Teo and Ruth both want to speak, but I'm not claiming it.

## P001: Teo

Teo keeps his hands where Sal can see them and says low to the others, "Ruth should do the talking. She's the talker, and I won't put a false word in this woman's ear, because I don't fake a reading, and that includes the ones I give people." He tells Cass and Ruth that I won't claim the parley roll or Totem, and that I accept Ruth speaking if Cass agrees. While Ruth parleys, he'll watch the ridge and the flats quietly with the compass, so we have a true bearing on the cellar whatever Sal says. If Ruth wants a Totem use, that's her call and her Luck.

## P001: Ruth

Ruth steps out in front of Cass and Teo, keeps her pistol holstered and her hands where Sal can see them. "Morning, Sal. We'll talk, but I'm not promising Redd a map or anything else I can't hand over. I'd like him to hear that we want the crew's name cleared, not a fight."

I'll parley, and I'm the speaker. Cass and Teo, I'm taking this one unless either of you objects. I want Sal to ride back with nothing, and my method is plain talk that doesn't promise anything. I'm worried she reads more from what I leave out than from what I say.

I'm using Totem/Feat before the roll, paid with 2 Luck and no Pressure, to cancel the mistrust Disadvantage. EMP 13 against her 11 is fair odds, and I'll decide on a nudge after I see the dice.

## G002: Custodian

Sal chews, head tilted, and lets Ruth finish. Her eyes go to what Ruth leaves out. Cass keeps her hands off the machete; Teo stands quiet with the compass low against his coat.

**Totem/Feat paid: 2 Luck (1 plus the Fracture surcharge), no Pressure.** The Advantage cancelled the mistrust Disadvantage, so Ruth rolled one die, and the Disadvantage is spent. Cass's and Teo's backing gave no Advantage: it is quiet support, not a second roller or a different method. Ruth's Luck is now 8.

**The dice (EMP, roll under):**
- Ruth, EMP 13: rolled **19**, a failure (margin -6).
- Sal, EMP 11: rolled **14**, a failure (margin -3).

Both failed, and the defender wins a double failure. As it stands, Sal wins: she rides back knowing more than you'd like, and Redd's crew will move faster.

**Ruth's choice, before I settle:**
- **Accept it.** No further cost. The parley result stands as above.
- **Nudge her die from 19 to 13 for 6 Luck** (1 per point). She would succeed while Sal fails, so Ruth wins: Sal rides back with nothing, and Redd's crew will be slower to move. That leaves Ruth at 2 Luck.
- Nudging Sal's die changes nothing useful. Nothing short of 6 points on Ruth's die changes the result. Nudges are paid from the roller's own pool.

The quarter hour passes either way.

Ruth, which is it?

## P002: Ruth

Ruth lets the die stand and keeps her face still. "Well, Sal, you heard a lot of nothing from me, and I'll let you take it back to Redd. I never promised him anything, and that's how it stays." I'm not spending Luck. Six of my eight tokens is too much for a result that only speeds Redd up, and I want the rest for what's coming. Cass, Teo, let's move, and Teo, what bearing does the compass give?

## G003: Custodian

Ruth declines the nudge, so the parley stands as it fell: Sal wins it.

Sal grins around her gum and swings onto her mule. "Nothing's all I needed, salvager." Her eyes slide to Teo's compass and the line it points, and she rides back up the scree. Within minutes the mule bells on the ridge quicken, and a rider peels off the skyline and drops out of sight behind it.

You walk on. Teo's bearing is true: the cellar lies straight across the flats, and the ridge keeps pace beside you. The Glass has turned hostile at Fracture. The air shimmers, grit hisses across the crust, and every step rings hollow.

Ahead, a field of fused glass, grey and blistered, spreads across your line. It sounds like it has a void under it. **A quarter hour of this first hour remains,** and the hour-end charge (+1, taking the track to 5) lands at the hour mark whichever you pick below.

**Options at the crust:**

1. **Cross fast.** REF, a few minutes. The walker who leads is your choice.
   - *Success:* you are across.
   - *Failure:* the lead walker falls through for 2 damage, and the coat's soak 1 applies, so 1 Stamina. Hauling them out costs the crew a quarter hour. This follows from the roll itself.
2. **Read a firm path.** INT, using Teo's counter or compass, a quarter hour. Teo is INT 12. The flicker-tech penalty is armed, so Teo rolls two dice and keeps the higher; it is spent when he tests and lost if the track resets first.
   - *Success:* a firm path, crossed with no further test.
   - *Failure:* the reading misleads and the crew must go round (the next option), losing the quarter hour already spent. This follows from the roll itself.
3. **Go round.** No test, half an hour.
4. **Something else.** Name it, and I will state its time and stakes first.

**Totem/Feat** is still unused by Cass and Teo. At Pressure 4 it costs 2 Luck, or 1 Luck and +1 Pressure (which tips the track to 5). Once the track resets to 0, it costs 1 Luck or +1 Pressure.

Who leads, or reads, or what else?

## P003: Cass

She pays out the rope behind her and says, "Teo, Ruth, stay on my line, ten paces back, and I'll test the crust with my heel as I go." Ask the Custodian: does paying out the rope behind me change anything if I go through, or is this just the plain REF test? I'll take the plain test if not, with no Totem and no Luck spent before the roll. If I fall, I'll take the 1 Stamina and the lost quarter hour.

## P003: Teo

I'll take the quarter hour and read a firm path with the counter and compass, and I'll give you the reading as it is, good or bad." I'm using Totem/Feat before the roll, paid with 2 Luck and no Pressure, to cancel the flicker-tech Disadvantage so I roll one die against INT 12. If the reading fails, I'll say so plainly and we go round, and I'm fine with Cass or Ruth calling which way if the Custodian has them roll.

## P003: Ruth

Ruth eyes the blistered crust and keeps her voice low. "Cass, you're the runner, so you lead the crossing fast. Teo and I will step where you step and keep our weight spread out." I'd rather not burn time on Teo's reading with the flicker-tech penalty armed, and a failed cross costs 1 Stamina and a quarter hour at worst. Cass, if you'd rather Teo read it, I'll go along with that. I'm not rolling this one, and I'm not spending Totem.

## G004: Custodian

**Cass's rope question.** Paying out the rope is good tradecraft, but it is a plain REF test. It gives no Advantage and does not change the stated failure, which already includes hauling the lead walker out for a quarter hour. If you go through, it is the same 1 Stamina and the same quarter hour.

## G005: Custodian

Cass goes out heel-first, rope paying out behind her, and the glass holds. Teo's few raps were a hunch and nothing more, and she picks her own line. The others step in her prints, and you reach the far edge without a crack.
