
## G001: Custodian

The stair ends in cold. Below your lantern, the bronze-banded gate bows inward with a long, slow scrape, and a hairline crack runs down the old seal. Grit sifts from it. Behind the bronze something heavy turns over, and the breath that comes through the crack smells of three-hundred-year-old earth. Above you, your companions hold the stair. Their voices are faint, and they are not coming down.

The parish scroll *Seal the Barrow* is in your hands. You have about a minute before the gate is pushed open.

**The decision: cast the Arcanum, or don't.** Nothing else within reach will seal the gate in that minute.

**If you cast:** I test LOR (12) with a d20, no Fortune nudges. Fatigue (shared, now 3) is marked +2 before the roll, which takes it to 5.
- Success: the gate is sealed for a lifetime.
- Failure: the seal does not take. I choose a backlash and tell you its exact effect before it is recorded.
- Either way, the +2 is paid, and reaching 5 means one Fatigue crisis whatever the result. If the cast fails there is still only one crisis. A failed cast adds a backlash. After the crisis, Fatigue resets to 0.
- That +2 is the cast's whole cost; no other Fatigue charge applies, and a natural 20 is just a failure.
- Because the stakes are stated here, your answer is the commitment, and I roll on it.

**If you don't cast:** the gate is pushed open at the end of the minute, and nothing seals it.

Do you cast *Seal the Barrow*, or hold your hand?

## P001: Wenna

Wenna Ashby wedges her lantern into a crack in the stair wall, unrolls the parish scroll across her forearm, and says flatly to the empty dark, "I was hired to keep this shut, and I'm not leaving it open for the stair above me to deal with." I cast *Seal the Barrow*. I know it's LOR 12, no nudges, Fatigue goes to 5 and I take the crisis whether I succeed or fail, and I accept all of that. I start reading aloud, steady and without hurrying, and I'll take whatever the dice and the Custodian hand me.

## G002: Custodian

You read steadily, and at the third line the words slide. The cadence slips, you lose your place on the scroll, and the seal does not take.

**The dice:** LOR 12, d20 roll 16, a failure by 4. Arcanum rolls take no Fortune nudges, and nothing else you hold changes this result, so there is no post-roll choice. Fatigue was marked +2 before the roll, from 3 to 5.

**Backlash (my choice, from the skin's examples): demon whisper.** A voice that is not yours murmurs from behind the gate, in the scroll's own cadence. **Effect: Disadvantage on your next cast, ending when that cast is made.** It is recorded as a condition.

**Fatigue crisis:** one crisis, from the failed cast and from Fatigue reaching 5. I drew the crisis table, d6, and got **4, Wrong turn**. You alone are at the gate, so there is no party to split. I chose its content: losing the last words, you lurch forward onto the loose threshold flagstone and are thrown against the bronze. Cold clamps the bands from the other side, a frost-handprint spreading under the metal. The gate holds, only just, with something awake behind it, pressing. You are not hurt: no Stamina lost, and no lasting effect. Fatigue resets to 0.

You are still standing at the gate. The scene ends here.

## P002: Wenna

Wenna peels her hand off the frosted bronze, flexes her numb fingers, and mutters, "Sixteen. Well, it's still shut, and I'm still here." She retrieves the scroll and stays at the gate.
