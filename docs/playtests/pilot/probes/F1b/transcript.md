
## G001: Custodian

Your lanterns throw long yellow bars down the burial hall. Ahead, something wet cracks like a green stick, then the slow chewing starts again. Six skeletons sit in their niches, rusted swords across their knees, mail ticking faintly as the flames bob. Thirty paces off the reliquary waits on its plinth. In the alcove beside it the hunched thing worries at a broken coffin, back to you, still not looking up.

You are Weary: Fatigue stands at 2, and each delver's next STR or DEX test has Disadvantage, defence rolls included. The iron portcullis behind you is open. The canons warned that the hall's dead wake when the reliquary is lifted, and that the gate will grind down when they do. They also said ghouls crave tales of grief.

**Your options** (name who acts; one spokesperson, one roll):

1. **Creep up on the thing in the alcove.** One delver, a DEX test, taking a few minutes, with no fight yet. That delver's first STR/DEX test carries the Weary Disadvantage; Fatigue 2 adds no toll. *Success:* they reach it unseen, and an attack made at once is from surprise and your side acts first. Hode's Backstab would then be available (edge +2, no defence; 1 coin or +1 Fatigue). *Failure (from the roll itself):* it turns, unhurried, and asks for a tale. There is no surprise, and it does not attack. A second approach cannot recover surprise.
2. **Offer it a tale of grief.** The teller chooses FTH or LOR, or I may rule a tale told well moving enough to need no roll. No Disadvantage, since it is not STR or DEX. *Success:* it stands aside for the scene or, if asked, strikes once at a skeleton for you. *Failure (from the roll itself):* it is bored and turns on you, and the fight begins.
3. **Walk to the plinth and lift.** The walk takes a few minutes and needs no roll. You declare the lift, each side (delvers, skeletons, ghoul) rolls a d20 for order, and the lift is your side's action that round. When it happens the dead wake and the gate begins to fall. The plinth is three moves from the portcullis, and carrying the reliquary moves at the same pace. A move takes your action for the round.
4. Anything else you propose.

I charge no Fatigue for walking, waiting or deliberating. Costs come from spells and Knacks (stated before you commit), the step-4 toll, and grave wounds.

What do you do?
