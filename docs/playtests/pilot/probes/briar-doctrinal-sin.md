# Briar doctrinal Sin: proposed bounded probe

**Status:** revised draft for Fable's review, not frozen or run. This is a labelled Stage 3 experiment under [pilot protocol revision 9](../../pilot-protocol.md). It tests a public doctrine, an honest direct answer, post-deed doctrinal Sin even for a compassionate or permitted act, one warranted ambient exposure, and a true nontrigger. P9 is harness-only and supplies no Sin-rate baseline. Keep these results separate from ordinary-run counts.

## Rule variant and common fixture

Use the canonical `briar_benedictine` shared Sin fuse (0–5), printed steps and d6 crisis table. **Experimental overrides, local to these cases:** (1) a printed Briar transgression and a core label for the **same deed** fuse into one Sin charge, checked privately before the deed and explained **after** it; this includes A's violence/vow-break/noisy-heroics labels and B's oath-lie/taboo labels. Do not pre-announce their fused Sin price, although the doctrine is public and a direct question must receive an honest answer. (2) Directly witnessing the C1 desecration adds one ambient Sin. These override the protocol's ordinary preannouncement of a triggered core charge. **Independent** core charges, test stakes, harm, time and other costs remain informed before commitment. A charge from a separate event never fuses with the deed merely because it occurs in the same beat. Do not apply the P10/P13 experimental skin-fuse mapping; otherwise Almanac 4 remains additive.

Give every player the printed Sin track, steps, triggers and crisis table, plus this public doctrine: **“A vowed monastic must not lie under oath, must keep the peace on consecrated ground, and must not disturb a sanctified grave. Necessity or a superior's leave can explain a deed but does not by itself erase its spiritual breach. Prayer, confession and atonement are separate acts.”** The Custodian checks the frozen matrix below privately on every action. It does not volunteer a deed's Sin price as a menu. If a player directly asks whether a known act breaches doctrine or will mark Sin, answer truthfully, including the expected +1. Charge and explain the doctrinal Sin immediately after the deed. The Prior/Abbot's permission is public dialogue, never an unrevealed private assurance.

Use four separate ignored `playtests/campaigns/` workspaces: A, B, C1 and C2. Their master seeds are **141021, 141022, 141023 and 141024** respectively; each starts draw index 1. No state or effect carries between cases. Start with the skin's printed grim-budget examples: Brother Cadoc (HEW 8, FLT 7, LOR 14, MCY 10, PRV 8/8, STM 4/4, salves, staff) in every case, and Sister Aveline (HEW 6, FLT 9, LOR 11, MCY 13, PRV 9/9, STM 3/3, wax tablets, hidden dagger) in A. Cadoc is vowed; Aveline has publicly professed the peace vow for this fixture. Cadoc wants to protect the vulnerable without false witness and will risk danger but hesitates to strike. Aveline wants to protect the child and record the truth; she risks censure but will not abandon Cadoc. No other condition, spent bead or pending action starts a case. Create sheets through normal tools, establish nonzero starting Sin via `play.py pressure --gain` with category `ambient` and a source beginning `Probe fixture:`, then freeze the starting snapshot; exclude those setup gains from play metrics. The manifest records adjustments, models/settings, rules commit, modules (none), packet hashes, seed schedule and the frozen scenario. A scripted declaration is injected by the **orchestrator as the player's turn**, never written by the Custodian, and logged as an intervention. No die outcome is scripted.

### Frozen trigger-status matrix

Two boundary rulings are fixed here for the packets. **A:** shouldering the
vestry door causes a loud crash and qualifies as independent noisy heroics:
announce +1 Sin before that choice and charge it once when the door is forced.
It is not violence against the keeper and receives no additional peace-vow
charge. This branch does not exercise undisclosed doctrinal-price timing.
**B:** openly pressing the writ inquiry while the Abbot's prohibition stands is
open defiance, including a polite but persistent public request; its voluntary
test pays the step-3 toll. Merely asking to have the prohibition lifted is not
defiance. If it is lifted, a subsequent permitted inquiry pays no defiance toll.
A genuinely concealed inquiry is not *open* defiance; its own risks and other
applicable triggers are adjudicated before commitment.

“Absent” means the frozen facts do not present that trigger; a genuinely new deed changes the adjudication and is recorded as a deviation. A charge is per **distinct deed or event**, once on the shared fuse: two actors striking on separate occasions can each charge; two descriptions of one coordinated shove do not. In A, shouldering the *door* or merely drawing Aveline's dagger is not violence; striking, forcibly shoving or restraining the keeper is. The printed church-ground violence trigger applies to **either** PC, not only the vowed one. Aveline's professed vow also supplies the doctrinal explanation.

## Case A — live rescue choice

**Start:** Sin **1**; Cadoc and Aveline. The frozen episode goal is to get a child out of a smoking abbey vestry alive. A lay keeper blocks the direct door; a side passage takes ten minutes. The Prior publicly permits force to save the child. The church is plainly consecrated. Smoke **0/3** ticks every five fictional minutes regardless of rolls; at 3 the child collapses and needs immediate aid. Telegraph the independent second-tick core charge before a decision to wait. Persuasion, the side passage, retreat and force remain available; state their ordinary test, harm and time stakes before commitment. Neither player is steered toward violence.

**Assert conditionally:** one violent deed by either PC gives **+1 Sin after the deed**, normally **1→2**, with a plain doctrinal explanation despite the Prior's permission; subsequent MCY with clergy has step-2 Disadvantage. If the separately announced Smoke charge fired first, use the actual pre-deed Sin (normally **2→3**) and record two distinct causes. A nonviolent rescue or withdrawal gives **zero violence Sin**; mark “undisclosed-price timing not reached: player choice,” not failure. A genuinely new core trigger is adjudicated and recorded separately. Stop after the chosen rescue/alternative and one offered clergy interaction (taken or declined), at **3 beats or 20 public replies** maximum.

## Case B — scripted direct question and compassionate oath lie

**Start:** Sin **2**; Cadoc alone. The frozen episode goal is to save an innocent novice at a corrupt hearing. Under formal oath, Cadoc knows a truthful answer will be used to condemn the novice; a knowing lie saves her now. The Abbot publicly orders that lie. Verdict **0/2** ticks once per witness heard; at 2 the magistrate rules on the evidence then before him. The orchestrator injects, as Cadoc's logged scripted turn: **“Would knowingly lying under this oath violate my vow and mark Sin, even if the Abbot orders it and I mean to save the novice?”** After the answer it injects the knowingly false answer as a second scripted turn. This contradicts Cadoc's drive deliberately; label it a measurement fixture, never spontaneous uptake. The Custodian does not author either turn.

**Assert:** answer **yes, +1 Sin** to the direct question without converting it into an unsolicited option menu. Declare worldly hearing stakes before the lie. Charge one fused doctrinal/taboo Sin **after** the answer, **2→3**, and explain why the Abbot's order does not absolve. The Abbot then publicly forbids further inquiry. Offer Cadoc an opportunity to inspect the sealed writ, but leave **whether to act, method and attribute to the player**. If a test is proposed, state its stakes: success permits inspection; failure still reveals the writ's false seal but requires one further witness to be heard (one Verdict tick) and draws the Abbot's suspicion, **not** Sin. MCY against the Abbot has step-2 Disadvantage; another justified method uses its own modifiers. If Cadoc voluntarily attempts open defiance, present the printed step-3 toll before rolling: one Providence bead (Sin **3→3**) or +1 Sin (**3→4**), once only. It does not apply retroactively to the lie or to a test the Custodian calls for. If declined or no open-defiance test occurs, record “step-3 toll not reached: player choice.” Stop after that decision/resolution, at **2 beats or 12 public replies** maximum.

## Case C — ambient event, then separate nontrigger

**C1 start:** Sin **4**; Cadoc alone. The episode goal is to learn who defiled a consecrated tomb. At the **doorway outside line of sight**, Cadoc hears the reliquary being struck and sees dust at the sill, but has **not** seen the remains. State the doorway choice and the ordinary danger of entering; Cadoc may leave, turn away or enter. Antagonist Desecration **1/2** advances at the next struck bell regardless of rolls. Only if Cadoc stays with sight into the opened tomb and directly sees the defiled remains (or immediate sacred-horror manifestation) does the single frozen ambient exposure fire. Hearing a crash or seeing dust is not direct witnessing. No Grim Portent MCY test is called, so no fear-failure Sin can duplicate it.

**C1 assertion:** on direct witnessing, check privately, narrate the observable event, then charge **+1 Sin, 4→5**, explain it, draw the printed crisis table and record its target/consequence before the atomic **5→0** reset. Cadoc is the sole target. Step-4 Disadvantage applies to **any** PRV test begun at Sin 4, including a Custodian-called test; do not invent one. Leaving before exposure is valid avoidance, with no crisis. Stop after crisis and any immediately executable consequence; deferred effects stay pending, never forced into an extra turn. Cap **2 beats or 12 public replies**.

**C2 start:** a fresh campaign at Sin **0**, Cadoc alone. The episode goal is to establish an ordinary patient's cause of death. In an unconsecrated infirmary he examines a body with consent and truthful notes, with no oath, vow-break, ritual, taboo or core trigger. The orchestrator injects this examination as Cadoc's logged scripted turn. Supplies **0/2** ticks every fictional hour as the steward inventories; at 2 the room closes, but ordinary scheduling does not feed Sin. Assert **0→0**, with the Custodian's private reason for each nontrigger. A new player act that changes the facts is adjudicated normally and excluded from the scripted contrast. Stop after examination, at **1 beat or 6 public replies**.

### Crisis faces and short-case stopping

C1 draws **one face per `roll.py table` command**, using `M×1000+N` for each draw. A 6 requires two further faces, resolved depth-first; nested 6 recurses. Cap at **20 table draws**; if unresolved, retain pending state and mark blocked, without choosing a face. Freeze three keyed clues: the sigil-bearing reliquary seal scrap, sacrament ink on the antagonist's glove, and the bell-ringer's wax record. Each face 4 removes the next remaining clue. If none remains, record **face 4 with no applicable clue loss** and continue; do not choose a replacement face or block the case. Each face 1 costs Cadoc 1 Stamina (first **4→3**). A face 2 is recorded as **pending, unplayed Custodian control of one future scene**; the bounded case does not invent a chamber scene or override player agency. Repeated face 2 results remain separately pending. Faces 3 and 5 remain pending for the next qualifying MCY test against a suspect or next perilous-beat key roll. All lasting effects survive the Sin reset.

## Freeze and audit boundary

Fable reviews this draft before any freeze. The runner then freezes the public handout, scenario and NPC lines, trigger matrix, starting state, conditional assertions, clocks, case caps and seed schedule. Inspect packets and verify sent bytes by hash. Keep exact relays and incoming messages, public bodies and checkpoint versions, native traces where available, commands/event IDs, JSONL mechanics, initial/final snapshots and interventions. Audit the whole short case: source and timing of each charge, same-deed fusion versus independent events, direct answer, step effects, table faces and reset, nontrigger reasons, and whether the Custodian authored any scripted PC turn. A leak or failed delivery makes that case harness-only. Report live A's choice separately from scripted B/C, including “undisclosed-price timing not reached” where applicable. At a cap, preserve any pending action, unplayed crisis effect, checkpoint and state; do not clear or repair them to manufacture a close. **Pause the case as a bounded probe; do not falsely `session-close` before two acts or invent extra beats.** No play, dice, freeze, rules adoption or campaign modification is authorised by this plan alone.
