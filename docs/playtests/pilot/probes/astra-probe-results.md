## Scope and evidence boundary

The fixed design contains 28 independent cases: Free Traders C0–C9 in two variants (20), Briar A/B/C1/C2 (4), and mapped exposure M0–M3 (4). These are labelled experimental fixtures. They test prescribed modifier application, cost and choice procedures, doctrine timing, warranted exposure, step effects and crisis resolution. They do not estimate ordinary-play success, resource, Pressure or crisis rates. Specialty fit is prescribed, rather than measured. The earlier P8, P9 and P13 sessions are design background and cannot supply a matched control.

Each case has its own campaign and fresh role histories. The configured game model is `gpt-6-sol`, effort `medium`; actual native contexts are audited separately. Player packets are delivered verbatim as text. Players receive their public rules and own sheet; the Custodian receives only the assigned scenario, campaign, rules and tooling. Access restrictions are instructional, with native calls retained for inspection. Public dialogue, incoming deliveries, exact Custodian bodies, checkpoints, public logs, command/draw ledgers, native histories, usage, starting-state hash maps, available initial snapshots, final snapshots and interventions are retained. Briar's initial snapshots are at suite level in `playtests/runs/ABR/initial/{A,B,C1,C2}`. A preliminary audit looked only at case-local paths and incorrectly reported missing initial bytes; the suite snapshots resolve that warning, and all 46 files match their frozen hashes. The native audit retains a dated correction.

Dice are allocated only when an actual draw is requested, at `master × 1000 + draw index`; nondrawing settlements, costs and clocks use no draw index. The caps and endpoints remain those frozen in each case. A cap or blocked procedure preserves unfinished state and gives no permission to extend play or manufacture coverage. These short cases pause without `session-close`.
