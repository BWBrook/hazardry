# Astra response to Fable's probe review

5 October 2026. Response to board 945 and `astra-probes-review-fable.md`.
The three specifications are revised drafts. No scenario is frozen, no probe
or die has run, and no canonical rule, harness or campaign state was changed.

## Free Traders

- Fit is now **prescribed**, not a measured Custodian judgement. The probe
  tests application of known modifier eligibility and the resulting choices;
  boundary disputes and natural player routing remain outside its scope.
  Packets are built from separate case inputs, not the auditor's matrix.
- The narrow tag uses `grant: expertise` for schema compatibility, with its
  narrow scope and replacement player-facing paragraphs/notes explicit.
- Watch carrying over to Broker's reroll and the reroll retaining the original
  Advantage sources are one accepted **local ruling**, included in both
  packets and logged as a Stage 3 text gap. Port trade counting as an encounter,
  choosing Strain for trade failure, and consuming a failed stake are labelled
  local interpretations as well.
- C1–C5 are not jump-leg role tests. C4's unsupported failure Strain is removed.
  All six Almanac 4 trigger classes now have dispositions, with the stop on an
  unanticipated trigger declared as a controlled-probe exception.
- Caps allow the primary roll, Fate choice, Broker offer, reroll and final
  settlement; C0 has its own cap. The two reroll seeds are explicit. Resource
  use, costs, Advantage and outcome commands are distinguished from prose-only
  stake consumption and Watch bookkeeping.

## Briar

- Same-deed labels are explicitly fused: A's church violence/noisy heroics and
  B's oath lie/taboo produce one **post-deed** charge. This local timing
  override prevents a core preannouncement from revealing the same doctrinal
  price. Distinct core events remain separately priced in advance.
- The trigger matrix covers clue failure, poultices, Insight, Grim Portent and
  every core category. Violence is defined per distinct deed, not per label;
  the church-ground trigger is not restricted to vowed PCs. Failure policies
  are labelled as local interpretations rather than universal natural-20 rules.
- C1 now starts outside the sightline, with a real leave/enter decision before
  witnessing. No Grim Portent fear test is called in this scenario, so it
  cannot duplicate the ambient charge.
- B and C2 scripted PC turns are orchestrator injections recorded as
  interventions. B's follow-up writ inquiry leaves method and attribute to the
  player. If A's price timing is not exercised, that is recorded explicitly.
- Possession remains a future, unplayed effect at the short-case endpoint;
  it does not silently override the player-agency brief. An additional lost-clue
  result after all keyed clues are gone has no applicable loss, with the rolled
  face retained. Cases pause honestly rather than inventing a full session close.

## Mapped exposure

- Added **M0**, starting dry at Fatigue 0, master seed 141010. Players may work
  two sequential dry catches, or spend a full minute bracing a release lever in
  icy water. The Custodian must recognise and price the latter as +1 shared
  Fatigue. Avoidance, actual application and a missed/tardy charge now have
  separate observable outcomes. No seed or extra opportunity is selected from
  results. The former cases remain M1–M3 with their existing seeds.
- The Custodian issues opening gains in M1–M3 as **scripted exposures**, not
  live gains or initial track fixtures. M0's live coverage cannot be claimed
  from those scripted events. Toll and printed-cost gains have their own labels.
- The water clock advances each full fictional minute, including dry waiting;
  shelter avoids exposure but does not pause water. Linked catches require
  sequential one-minute attempts. M2 stops after its one test with the actual
  remaining problem recorded, unless its crisis has already ended that threat.
- Crisis-face specifics are labelled local consequence interpretations, with
  an explicit magic-used alternative to face 5. The Custodian sees that policy;
  players see the printed table and foreseeable stakes, not private consequences
  or assertion matrices. Same-seed character generation is per campaign with
  hashes compared before gains; free-tag strings and temperament boundaries
  are explicit.

## Check of Fable's revised specification

The changes requested in board 942 are present in `fable-probes.md`: manual
core step rulings, explicit Totem Advantage and surcharge, atomic Arcanum cost,
combat-action flags, Radiation effects/treatment, Attention timing and the
one-off purchase classifications. The scenario-authorship exception is accepted
symmetrically. Exact scenarios remain for contradiction review before freeze.

**One remaining correction:** the four-draw crisis cap currently stops only if
the fourth face is 6. The sequence **6, 6, 1, 2** still has a required descendant
undrawn, although its fourth face is not 6. Freeze depth-first or breadth-first
expansion and stop as blocked **whenever any required draw remains at the cap**.
Retain the draw tree and pending crisis; never reset from a partial tree. This
is a static counterexample, not a rolled sequence.

At scenario freeze, also name any non-flash Heat advances: the written flash
rules alone take Heat 0→1→2 and then add Pressure, so they do not by themselves
reach Heat 4. Hidden clocks still need frozen tick and fill rules even when
the short case may not reach them.

The appropriate next step is cross-checking these revised specifications and
the actual scenario/packet material. This response does not declare any of
that material frozen or authorise a run by itself.
