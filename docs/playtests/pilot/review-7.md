## What revision 8 fixed

- **Setup checks worked.** The pre-freeze contradiction check sent four points back to the Custodian, all resolved before the freeze. Packet inspection caught a sentence that gave a hidden clock's rate.
- **Help rulings worked.** Both requests got a stated ruling, with its reason and "does not stack", before the dice.
- **Records worked.** One writer per transcript; all 19 checkpoints and all 45 deliveries match. The closing ran as written: closing lines, one archive, literal receipts.
- **Agency held.** The Custodian never wrote a player character's words, treated one player's orders to the others as suggestions, and asked each directly.
- **Frozen consequences mostly held.** It refused a post-roll rewrite of a bargain ("the stakes were fixed before the dice") and answered "is there another way out?" honestly. The exception is G014–G016: the fresh offer bypassed failure stakes that had sounded automatic (item 4 below).
- **A short ending stayed honest.** Seven beats resolved the haul, the ownership dispute, the treatment and the dusk deadline. Almanac 9's numbers are typical, not quotas; padding would have been worse.

## What still fails

**1. Narrator summaries and menus are a recurring leak route.** Two runs of the second tranche are harness tests only for private NPC lines:
- P9's narration stated one;
- P11's "Where it stands" summary stated another (Wren), and a later reply claimed she had said it.

(P3's earlier harness-test status was a different kind of breach: it exposed a published crisis table outside the old packet boundary, and current packets include those tables.) Revision 7 already forbids publishing private lines. The failure is structural: text in the narrator's voice turns private notes into stated fact. G002 shows the same pattern for clocks: it forecast the riders' hidden timetable while announcing the cost of staying.

**2. Frozen triggers are missed, not just avoided.** P11's flare matched frozen policy line 15 and drew no charge, warning or exemption. That makes P11's Pressure 0 unclean. The Custodian applied the narrower rival-clock trigger correctly and missed the broader Pressure line beside it.

**3. Peril errors still run both ways.** P13 over-counted under revision 7 (a split scene), and two runs under-counted:
- P10 beat 6 (a timed swap that risked the goal);
- P11 beat 5 (an NPC's life, saved without a test).

P11 beat 6 depends on how the episode goal is scoped, and nothing at setup said. No milestone was awarded in P10 or P11, though one was eligible in both on at least one reading. These are particular errors; they do not show that the stricter wording caused a general downward shift.

**4. The Custodian improvised a remedy for its own error.** At G014 a diagnostic command drew a real roll without the agreed Advantage. Disclosure and keeping the dice were right. The two-Luck rebate was an invented rule, and the fresh offer bypassed failure stakes (arbitration, an hour, a sound case) that had sounded automatic. Nothing in the brief covers a Custodian's own procedural error.

**5. An offered option was narrowed without cause.** G008 dropped G007's all-three-to-Pell option during a role collision. Revision 8's "roll or role" line covers the collision; the narrowing was an application error. I withdrew my first-named-volunteer proposal, which Astra showed was not needed.

**6. Carried gear went stale.** Rue's sheet still lists a burned-out flare and the rope left in the cellar.

## Proposals: protocol revision 9, for the probes

**A. Summaries carry only public facts, with their source.** Add to the Custodian brief: "In any summary, state an NPC's position only as something they said or did on screen, and say who or what it came from ('Pell says Vane honours the earliest paper'). Never put an NPC's private line, a hidden clock's schedule or an unrevealed motive in a summary, even as a likely outcome." Mirror it in the handbook.

**B. Check frozen triggers on every declared action.** Add: "Before resolving a declared action, check it against every frozen trigger. If one applies, state the charge before the action is committed, and record it when it happens. If you judge that a trigger does not apply, say why in your private notes." (P11 flare.) **Exception:** the agreed Briar doctrinal-Sin experiment (review 5) keeps its own timing. The vows and doctrine are public and questions are answered honestly, but Sin is assessed and explained after the deed, and a superior's permission does not absolve it automatically. The Custodian still checks the trigger before resolution. Other costs and dice stakes stay informed.

**C. Peril: lives and goals.** Add two worked lines to the perilous-beat item:
- "A scene in which an NPC's life hangs on the outcome is perilous, even with no test" (P11 beat 5).
- At setup, the frozen scenario states the initial episode goal in one line, as the audit reference for the goal limb (P11 beat 6). Players may change the goal; record a genuine change when it happens, never afterwards to justify a flag.

**D. The Custodian's own errors.** Add to the brief and the handbook: "If you make a procedural error, disclose it, keep any dice drawn, and offer only what the printed rules allow. Never invent compensation. If the rules cannot repair it, record it and play on only if the campaign state permits a valid continuation. A pending action or crisis cannot be bypassed, cleared by hand or rerolled to make progress; if no supported recovery exists, preserve the evidence and stop that case as blocked." A probe label never authorises repairing drawn dice. Add for the Custodian's diagnostic habits: "Never run a play command to test its syntax; use `--help`. `--dry-run` still draws dice."

**E. Stakes say what triggers them.** Extend the stakes-before-commitment line: "Say whether a failure consequence follows from the roll itself or from refusing the offer, so everyone knows whether a new approach can avert it" (G014–G016).

**F. Keep offered options.** Add: "Once you have offered an option, keep it unless the fiction changes, and say what changed" (G008).

**G. Close with the gear.** Turn-loop step 6: before the closing reply, the Custodian reconciles consumed or abandoned gear on each sheet with `update_sheet.py`, and says so in the public log.

**H. Probe framing.** Each probe states before the freeze:
- the procedures it must reach;
- why the fiction warrants that exposure;
- a stop condition, which may be shorter than a session.

Each case also freezes its starting conditions, exact assertions, variant and cap. Any prepared fixture (a high track, a forced failure) is labelled. No outcome-hunting rerolls. A probe may use separate bounded subcases rather than stretch one scene to reach every item. Probe results are labelled as probes and never pooled with ordinary-run counts.

## Harness fix before the probes

**Tableless crisis (Astra's P2, reproduced).** `skins/core.md` correctly says the core prints no crisis table. However, `play.py` and `_pressure.py` require `--table-result` with d6 faces, so an honest core crisis is rejected and the next action blocks. The only way through is a fabricated face.

**Proposed fix (amended by Astra, board 934):**
- the manifest marks skins whose crisis table is optional (`crisis_table_required: false`, on `core` today; the default stays required);
- for those skins, `pressure --crisis` accepts an omitted `--table-result` and records `table_result: []`, with no draw;
- a supplied result is still accepted and validated as d6 faces, because a campaign may freeze its own table (P11 did, policies lines 22–29);
- skins with a printed table keep the current requirement;
- the target-and-consequence-before-reset contract is unchanged, in both `play.py` and `_pressure.crisis`; no table registry or override mechanism is added;
- tests cover: a tableless reset, its cycle and effect retention; custom-table results and invalid values; the unchanged pending, target and consequence guards; event-ID replay and changed-request rejection; and the required-table regression.

This fix supports any future core crisis, not only the probes. It is a source change, so it needs Barry's approval.

**Not proposed now (Stage 4 backlog):**
- a validation-only mode that draws no dice;
- clearer errors for unresolved NPC names (`npc:NAME` is already in `opposed --help`);
- voiding a mistaken pending roll while keeping the original event, dice, reason and replacement provenance.

Proposal D covers the immediate risk without these.
