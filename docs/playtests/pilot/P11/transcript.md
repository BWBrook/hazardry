At the dusk bell the shutters of the Scale House come down. Hale's counter arch ticks over the Ash Gate as the lamps go up. Case Five sits in the Wardens' hot store under its lead tag, and Teague's wagons rumble out of Market Row with no Blue Salt aboard. Teague himself stops at the Wash door long enough to take one look at Pell and say nothing.

Wren sits on a stool by Pell's cot with her arms folded and her scrip counted a third time. Rue sits beside her. Sula adds the eight doses to her list of thirty names and does not say thank you. She says, "Come back hot, and the Wash sees you right," and goes to find a lamp.

In the small hours Pell's breathing eases, and the needle on Hollis's counter comes off the pin. "He'll see the morning," Sula says, and goes back to her list.
