## G001: Custodian

**Wyckmere Abbey, 12 December 1142. Dawn, sleet, hard frost. MI: the mill-leat.**

Alys the miller's wife is screaming from the bank. Out on the ice by the sluice, Wat, the ten-year-old whose grandfather has been missing for days, is on his belly clawing at a dark shape under the ice. It is a coat, a pale hand, a face. The ice by the sluice groans and spiderwebs under him; black water shows at one edge.

Up the meadow from the abbey wall comes Lay Brother Wulfric, the cellarer's under-porter, limping hard, with two lay-brothers and a hurdle. "To carry the dead to the sexton," he calls, and he is not stopping. The miller's boy has fetched you both, Brother Anselm from the almonry gate, Sister Hawise from the guest-house. The leat is outside the wall, on abbey land but not consecrated ground.

There is a coil of mill-rope on the bank, and Anselm's ash staff.

**The ice will not wait.** If you stand and argue, it goes, and Wat with it. There is one test to get him and the body to the bank, and one roller. You choose who rolls and by which method:

1. **Fleet (FLT): lie flat, push the staff or rope out to him and draw him in.** Anselm FLT 10, Hawise FLT 6.
2. **Hew (HEW): haul from the bank on the mill-rope, others bracing, and drag the body in on the line.** Anselm and Hawise both HEW 9.

The other one of you may anchor the rope or the staff from the bank, which gives the roller Advantage (roll two d20, keep the lower).

Roll d20 under the attribute; the margin is attribute minus roll. Afterwards, Providence can nudge the die by 1 per bead (Anselm 7, Hawise 9), and a natural 1 or 20 cannot be nudged.

- **Success:** Wat and the body are on the bank in your hands, before Wulfric's men reach it.
- **Failure:** you get the boy out, but you go in to the waist or fall hard. Lose 1 Stamina to the cold, and Wulfric's lay-brothers have the body on the hurdle before you can stop them. You can still speak to them.

Hawise's rosemary and yarrow and Anselm's satchel are to hand, if anyone is hurt afterwards. Nobody has been struck yet, and nothing has been decided about what you say to Wulfric.

Who goes out, and by which method?
