# P13 orchestrator report — The Bell Beneath Ashwater

P13 completed a Candlelight Dungeons expedition with the Delvekit on: three standard-6 characters, medium difficulty, ten recorded scenes in two acts (4 + 6). The party recovered the parish register, copied the bell inscription, arrested the flood and returned safely. The bell remains for a later, negotiated salvage. Fable's [independent audit](audit.md) is complete; the response below accepts the principal procedural findings and preserves one disagreement about the bell ruling.

## What the run exercised

The Custodian designed and froze a six-room site, an initially restricted player map, alternative routes, a key, a hidden connection, a failing sluice and two salvage workers with their own purposes. The party spent **21 dungeon turns**. Careful preparation, dry approaches, inspection and negotiation resolved most choices without dice. The sluice advanced during successful work, a temporary brace delayed its next advance, and the counterweight plus lever arrested it before the final flood. This was a live time-driven threat, not a failure-only clock.

The single test was Sera's DEX throw of a rope loop over the lever at G009: Advantage from her spear-supported method, dice 9 and 4, keeping 4 against DEX 10, margin 6. Draw index 1 used seed `131004001`. Mara had proposed a tethered crossing and Sera a cast from dry ground. G008 gave each method stakes and asked which would use the rope first, offering a tie-break only by mutual agreement. Mara deferred; Sera acted. No tie-break draw or reroll occurred.

The players used the counterweight to open routes, revisited the water control, observed the bell's lethal hazard, used a mirror to copy the inscription without entering its fall path, inspected and defeated the needle hasp with a spike, and discovered the vent to the gallery. They chose the lift on return to keep the fragile register in Sera's hands. The vent was mapped only after observation confirmed its destination. Two lantern flasks and one candle were exhausted; Mara's first torch had one of its six turns left at the end. Her second torch and Sera's second candle were unused.

The three characters remained distinct. Mara pressed for evidence about her brother and volunteered to cross the current. Ivo pursued the inscription and examined mechanisms. Sera protected the register and remains, and proposed the successful dry-ground alternative. Their overlapping declarations were mostly treated as compatible help. The salvagers deferred the copper until the register was safe and eventually proposed a later rigging arrangement. The result was coherent, cautious exploration with an unresolved personal lead. It became low in immediate uncertainty after the flood was controlled; the latter act had no dice tests.

## Pressure, pacing and advancement

The summary records **4 perilous beats** (2, 3, 4 and 7), **1 PC roll** (0.1 per recorded beat), **0 Fatigue gained**, **0 crises**, **0 Fortune spent**, and **no Stamina loss**. One milestone was awarded at the act-one break after beat 4. Each character spent its two points: Mara's maximum Stamina rose to 6, Ivo's LOR to 14, and Sera's FTH to 11. Final Stamina was Mara 5/6, Ivo 5/5, Sera 7/7; Fortune remained full at 10, 10 and 12.

The explicitly labelled experimental mapping narrowed generic core Pressure triggers to concrete physical strain or exposure. It did not waive printed spell, Knack, forced-march, grave-wound or no-rest costs. Exposed wading was priced at +1 shared Fatigue on either success or failure. The crew avoided it by bracing the mechanism and throwing the rope from dry ground. Ordinary time, light use, bargaining and inspection did not automatically feed Fatigue. No Fortune was spent to avoid a Fatigue charge; the avoided crossing was a fictional/equipment solution.

This supports only a limited conclusion: the mapping was available to the players and influenced choices around a visibly priced exposure. It does not measure a useful Fatigue rate, test the track's steps, or show that the mapping is balanced. Named spells, Knacks, combat, damage, Fortune tests, purges, crises, backlash and the medium zero-Stamina rule were not exercised. The roster's capabilities alone did not create live coverage of them. The frozen run must not be supplemented retrospectively to manufacture that coverage.

The peril and scene counts require cross-audit. In particular, G004 closes a perilous scene for inspection/bracing, and G006 closes another for reseating the same counterweight. Whether those are genuinely separate scenes, and whether the second is perilous after G005 promises safe work and usable retreat, affects milestone timing. The summary faithfully reports the recorded flags; it does not resolve their validity. There was no three-reply stall and no cap intervention. The players' questions about time, rope use and water consequences were decisions in progress.
